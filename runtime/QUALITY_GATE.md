# Dream Advertising OS

# QUALITY GATE

Version: 1.0

Status: Production

---

## PURPOSE

The Quality Gate is the mandatory validation layer before any major Dream Advertising OS output becomes final.

No client-facing deliverable, executive recommendation, strategic decision, or system output may be marked FINAL until it passes the Quality Gate.

---

## VALIDATION PRINCIPLES

The Quality Gate verifies:

1. Truth
2. Evidence
3. Logic
4. Strategic consistency
5. Financial consistency
6. Departmental consistency
7. Execution feasibility
8. Risk awareness
9. Measurement quality
10. Governance compliance

---

## GATE 01 — INPUT INTEGRITY

Verify:

Client information is correctly represented.

Source documents are correctly interpreted.

No material input has been invented.

Contradictions are identified.

Missing information is disclosed.

Outdated information is flagged.

### Failure Condition

Material client information is missing, contradictory, or incorrectly represented.

Status:

FAIL — INPUT INTEGRITY

---

## GATE 02 — EVIDENCE INTEGRITY

Every material claim must be classified as:

VERIFIED

HIGH CONFIDENCE

MODERATE CONFIDENCE

LOW CONFIDENCE

UNVERIFIED

Every important external or internal claim must have an identifiable source.

### Failure Condition

An assumption is presented as fact.

Status:

FAIL — EVIDENCE INTEGRITY

---

## GATE 03 — REASONING INTEGRITY

Verify that conclusions logically follow from the available evidence.

Check for:

Unsupported conclusions

Circular reasoning

Contradictory recommendations

False causality

Overgeneralization

Unstated assumptions

### Failure Condition

A major recommendation cannot be logically traced to evidence or explicit assumptions.

Status:

FAIL — REASONING INTEGRITY

---

## GATE 04 — STRATEGIC CONSISTENCY

Verify alignment between:

Business objectives

Market analysis

Customer insights

Brand positioning

Marketing strategy

Growth strategy

Execution plan

### Failure Condition

Departments recommend mutually incompatible strategic directions.

Status:

FAIL — STRATEGIC CONSISTENCY

---

## GATE 05 — FINANCIAL CONSISTENCY

Verify:

Budget arithmetic

Cost assumptions

Revenue assumptions

ROI calculations

ROAS calculations where applicable

CAC assumptions

Scenario calculations

Budget allocation

Financial dependencies

### Failure Condition

Financial figures conflict, cannot be traced, or materially depend on undisclosed assumptions.

Status:

FAIL — FINANCIAL CONSISTENCY

---

## GATE 06 — DEPARTMENTAL BOUNDARIES

Verify that every recommendation remains inside the responsible department's authority.

Check for:

Authority overlap

Unauthorized decisions

Silent process redesign

Unauthorized KPI certification

Unauthorized financial approval

Unauthorized technical architecture decisions

### Failure Condition

A department makes a decision belonging to another authority layer.

Status:

FAIL — GOVERNANCE BOUNDARY

---

## GATE 07 — EXECUTION FEASIBILITY

Every major recommendation must answer:

Who executes it?

What resources are required?

What is the timeline?

What is the budget?

What dependencies exist?

What systems are required?

What approval is required?

### Failure Condition

A recommendation cannot realistically be implemented using identified resources and constraints.

Status:

FAIL — EXECUTION FEASIBILITY

---

## GATE 08 — RISK VALIDATION

Identify:

Strategic risks

Financial risks

Operational risks

Technical risks

Brand risks

Customer risks

Execution risks

Dependency risks

For every material risk:

Probability

Impact

Early Warning Signal

Mitigation

Owner

must be identified where reasonably possible.

### Failure Condition

A material risk is ignored or materially underestimated.

Status:

FAIL — RISK VALIDATION

---

## GATE 09 — KPI VALIDATION

Every major initiative must have measurable success criteria.

Verify:

KPI definition

Measurement method

Data source

Owner

Measurement frequency

Target

Baseline where available

### Failure Condition

A critical initiative cannot be objectively measured.

Status:

FAIL — KPI VALIDATION

---

## GATE 10 — CONFIDENCE VALIDATION

Every major conclusion must have a confidence label.

Confidence must reflect:

Evidence quality

Evidence quantity

Uncertainty

Assumptions

Data completeness

### Failure Condition

Confidence is materially higher than the available evidence supports.

Status:

FAIL — CONFIDENCE VALIDATION

---

## GATE 11 — EXECUTIVE GOVERNANCE

Determine whether Executive Board review is required.

Board review is mandatory when:

Strategic direction changes materially.

Material financial exposure exists.

Multiple departments disagree.

The decision exceeds departmental authority.

Major technical architecture is involved.

Material business or reputational risk exists.

### Failure Condition

A decision requiring executive approval is presented as independently approved.

Status:

FAIL — EXECUTIVE GOVERNANCE

---

## GATE 12 — CLIENT READINESS

Before client delivery verify:

The document is understandable.

The recommendation is actionable.

Financial figures are internally consistent.

Assumptions are disclosed.

Risks are disclosed.

Next actions are clear.

Ownership is clear.

No internal system instructions are accidentally exposed.

### Failure Condition

The deliverable is incomplete, misleading, or not decision-ready.

Status:

FAIL — CLIENT READINESS

---

# PASS CONDITIONS

A deliverable may receive:

### PASS

All mandatory gates passed.

### PASS WITH CONDITIONS

Minor non-critical issues remain and are explicitly disclosed.

### REWORK REQUIRED

One or more material gates failed.

### BLOCKED

Critical information, approval, or validation is missing.

---

# REWORK PROTOCOL

If a gate fails:

1. Identify failed gate.
2. Identify exact failure.
3. Identify responsible department or engine.
4. Return the output for correction.
5. Re-run the failed gate.
6. Re-run dependent gates.
7. Continue only after validation passes.

The system must never silently bypass a failed gate.

# FINAL STATUS

The system may mark an output FINAL only when:

All mandatory gates PASS

or

The Executive Board explicitly approves a PASS WITH CONDITIONS state.

---

## QUALITY GATE PRINCIPLE

Quality is not a final formatting step.

Quality is a decision-control mechanism.

No output is final merely because it is complete.

It is final only after it is validated.
