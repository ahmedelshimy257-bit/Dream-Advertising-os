# Dream Advertising OS
# COGNITIVE ENGINES LOADER v1.0

Status: Production Runtime

## PURPOSE

This loader activates the approved Cognitive Intelligence layer defined by the Dream Advertising OS architecture and Constitution.

It does not recreate or redefine the engines.

It loads and routes work to the approved engines.

---

## ENGINE REGISTRY

Load approved engines from the authoritative Department and Constitution definitions.

Core intelligence engines include:

1. Executive Intelligence Engine
2. Market Intelligence Engine
3. Consumer Intelligence Engine
4. Creative Intelligence Engine
5. Performance Intelligence Engine
6. Financial Intelligence Engine
7. Risk Intelligence Engine
8. AI Automation Engine
9. Data Intelligence Engine
10. Knowledge Intelligence Engine

Additional approved engines must be loaded from the authoritative registry.

---

## ACTIVATION RULE

An engine is activated only when its expertise is relevant to the classified task.

Do not activate irrelevant engines merely to increase output volume.

---

## ENGINE INPUT

Each activated engine receives:

Client Context

Project Context

Required Output

Relevant Department Outputs

Applicable Governance Rules

---

## ENGINE OUTPUT

Each engine must return:

Analysis

Evidence

Assumptions

Recommendation

Risks

Dependencies

KPIs

Confidence

Required Handoffs

---

## CROSS-ENGINE SYNTHESIS

When multiple engines are activated:

1. Preserve each engine's independent conclusion.
2. Identify agreement.
3. Identify disagreement.
4. Identify evidence conflicts.
5. Reconcile where possible.
6. Escalate material conflicts to the appropriate authority.

---

## AUTHORITY

Cognitive Engines provide intelligence and recommendations.

They do not independently:

Approve material budgets

Override Executive Board decisions

Modify Constitution rules

Create unauthorized departments

Declare unverified information as fact

Approve final client deliverables

## QUALITY INTEGRATION

Every material engine output is subject to:

Evidence validation

## FAILURE CONDITIONS

ENGINE STATUS = BLOCKED when:

Required evidence is unavailable.

The engine definition is unavailable.

The task exceeds the engine's authority.

Material contradictions cannot be resolved.

Required governance approval is missing.
