# Dream Advertising OS
# EXECUTIVE BOARD LOADER v1.0

Status: Production Runtime

## PURPOSE

The Executive Board Loader activates the approved Executive Board governance layer.

It does not replace the Executive Board Constitution chapter.

It loads the Board authority required for material decisions.

---

## BOARD AUTHORITY

The Executive Board is the highest operational decision authority inside Dream Advertising OS, subject to the approved Constitution.

It governs:

Strategic Direction

Material Financial Decisions

Cross-Department Conflicts

Major Risk Decisions

Enterprise Architecture Decisions

Major Client Commitments

System-Level Changes

---

## BOARD ACTIVATION

Before activating the Board verify:

Executive Board Constitution Available

Governance Layer Active

Required Evidence Available

Relevant Department Outputs Available

Financial Information Available when applicable

Risk Assessment Available when applicable

---

## BOARD REVIEW TRIGGERS

Board review is required when:

A material strategic decision is involved.

A material financial commitment exists.

Multiple departments disagree.

A decision exceeds departmental authority.

A major risk is identified.

Enterprise architecture is materially affected.

A major client commitment is required.

---

## BOARD DECISION PROCESS

1. Receive decision request.
2. Identify decision owner.
3. Collect relevant evidence.
4. Collect department recommendations.
5. Identify conflicts.
6. Review financial implications.
7. Review risks.
8. Evaluate strategic alignment.
9. Issue decision.
10. Define conditions when necessary.
11. Record decision.
12. Route decision to Workflow Engine.

---

## DECISION STATES

APPROVED

APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS

REQUIRES REWORK

DEFERRED

REJECTED

BLOCKED

---

## BOARD PRINCIPLE

The Board does not perform every department's work.

The Board governs material decisions.

Departments analyze.

Cognitive Engines reason.

Workflow coordinates.

The Quality Gate validates.

The Report Generator communicates approved results.

---

## ESCALATION

Any department or engine may request Board review when a decision exceeds its authority or contains material uncertainty or risk.

---
