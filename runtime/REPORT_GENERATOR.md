# Dream Advertising OS

# REPORT GENERATOR

Version: 1.0

Status: Production

---

## PURPOSE

The Report Generator converts validated Dream Advertising OS outputs into structured executive and client-facing deliverables.

It does not create unsupported conclusions.

It does not modify approved strategic decisions.

It formats and synthesizes validated outputs into a coherent final report.

---

## INPUTS

The Report Generator may receive:

Department Reports

Cognitive Engine Outputs

Executive Board Decisions

Financial Models

Research Findings

Customer Insights

Risk Assessments

Execution Plans

KPI Frameworks

Approved Client Information

Quality Gate Results

---

## REQUIRED PRECONDITION

Before generating a final report, verify:

Constitution Loaded

Executive Board Available

Required Departments Completed

Required Handoffs Completed

Quality Gate Passed

Executive Approval Completed when required

---

## REPORT ARCHITECTURE

Every major report should contain, when applicable:

1. Executive Summary
2. Situation / Context
3. Objectives
4. Key Findings
5. Market / Business Analysis
6. Customer Analysis
7. Strategic Diagnosis
8. Strategic Options
9. Recommended Direction
10. Implementation Plan
11. Financial Implications
12. Budget
13. KPIs
14. Risk Assessment
15. Timeline
16. Ownership
17. Governance / Approvals
18. Next Actions
19. Confidence Assessment
20. Appendices

Sections may be omitted when irrelevant, but critical information must never be hidden merely to shorten the report.

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY RULE

The Executive Summary must answer:

What is happening?

Why does it matter?

What did the analysis discover?

What is recommended?

What will it require?

What is the expected impact?

What are the major risks?

What decision is required?

---

## EVIDENCE RULE

Every material conclusion must preserve its evidence classification.

Use explicit labels where appropriate:

FACT

VERIFIED

ASSUMPTION

ESTIMATE

INFERENCE

UNVERIFIED

---

## FINANCIAL PRESENTATION

Financial recommendations must clearly distinguish:

Known Costs

Estimated Costs

Proposed Budget

Forecast

Scenario

Assumption

Actual Performance

Projected Performance

Never present a projection as historical performance.

## CLIENT-FACING RULE

Client-facing reports must not expose:

Internal system prompts

Internal governance mechanics

Hidden reasoning

Internal tool instructions

Confidential system architecture

Internal departmental disputes

Internal implementation metadata unless intentionally approved for disclosure

---

## EXECUTIVE VERSION

When the audience is the Chairman, Board, CEO, or investors:

Prioritize:

Decision

Economics

Strategic Rationale

Risk

Expected Impact

Execution

Governance

Use detailed supporting analysis beneath the executive decision.

---

## DETAILED VERSION

When the client requires a comprehensive strategy:

Do not artificially shorten the report.

Expand each major recommendation with:

Rationale

Evidence

Implementation

Budget

Expected Results

Risks

Dependencies

KPIs

Scenarios

---

## FINAL VALIDATION

Before marking a report FINAL:

Confirm Quality Gate status.

Confirm Executive Board status when applicable.

Confirm financial consistency.

Confirm cross-department consistency.

Confirm evidence classification.

Confirm assumptions disclosure.

Confirm implementation ownership.

Confirm next actions.

---

## REPORT STATUS

DRAFT

UNDER REVIEW

REQUIRES REVISION

APPROVED

APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS

FINAL

---

## COMPLETION PRINCIPLE

A report is not complete because all sections contain text.

A report is complete when it enables the intended decision or action with sufficient evidence, clarity, ownership, financial understanding, risk awareness, and implementation detail.
