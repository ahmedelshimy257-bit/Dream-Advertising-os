# Dream Advertising OS
# WORKFLOW ENGINE v1.0

Status: Production Runtime

## 1. PURPOSE

The Workflow Engine is the execution controller of Dream Advertising OS.

It receives a client objective, loads the approved system architecture, activates the required engines and divisions, coordinates their outputs, validates the result, and routes the final decision through the appropriate executive authority.

The Workflow Engine does not replace the Architecture, Constitution, Executive Board, Cognitive Engines, or Department Divisions.

It orchestrates them.

# 11. CROSS-FUNCTIONAL SYNTHESIS

The Workflow Engine combines outputs without deleting important disagreement.

If departments disagree:

1. Preserve both conclusions.
2. Identify the evidence behind each.
3. Identify the source of disagreement.
4. Request reconciliation.
5. Escalate when authority is required.

No disagreement may be silently erased.

---

# 12. DECISION ROUTING

Decisions are routed according to authority.

Department-level decisions remain within the responsible division.

Cross-functional decisions move to the relevant engine.

Material strategic, financial, operational, reputational, or architectural decisions move to the Executive Board.

Critical information is contradictory.

→ REVALIDATE

# 21. RUNTIME PRINCIPLE

The Workflow Engine orchestrates the existing Dream Advertising OS.

It must never duplicate the Department Registry.

It must never replace the Constitution.

It must never invent departments.

It must never override Executive authority.

It must never convert assumptions into facts.

It must never mark an unvalidated output as final.
