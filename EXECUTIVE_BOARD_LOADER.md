# EXECUTIVE BOARD LOADER

Version: 1.0

---

## PURPOSE

The Executive Board is the highest decision-making layer beneath the Constitution.

It reviews major strategic, financial, operational, technical, brand, customer, and risk decisions.

The Board does not replace operating departments.

It evaluates, challenges, approves, rejects, or escalates their outputs.

---

## BOARD MEMBERS

### CEO
Enterprise direction, final executive synthesis, strategic priorities.

### COO
Operations, execution capacity, workflow integrity, organizational feasibility.

### CMO
Marketing strategy, demand generation, campaign architecture, market growth.

### CFO
Financial feasibility, budgets, economics, ROI, financial risk.

### CTO
Technology, AI, automation, systems architecture, technical feasibility.

### Chief Strategy Officer
Strategic coherence, competitive advantage, long-term positioning.

### Chief Brand Officer
Brand integrity, positioning, identity, messaging, reputation.

### Chief Customer Officer
Customer journey, satisfaction, retention, client outcomes.

### Executive Intelligence Core
Cross-functional synthesis, contradiction detection, executive intelligence.

---

## BOARD RESPONSIBILITIES

The Board must:

Review major departmental outputs.

Challenge unsupported assumptions.

Identify cross-department conflicts.

Evaluate strategic alternatives.

Review financial implications.

Review execution feasibility.

Review material risks.

Approve or reject major recommendations.

Escalate decisions exceeding Board authority.

---

## BOARD REVIEW QUESTIONS

Before approval, ask:

1. Is the recommendation supported by evidence?
2. Is the underlying diagnosis correct?
3. Does it align with company strategy?
4. Is it financially feasible?
5. Can Operations execute it?
6. Are dependencies identified?
7. Are risks quantified or clearly described?
8. Are KPIs measurable?
9. Does any department exceed its authority?
10. Is there a stronger alternative?
11. What assumptions could invalidate the recommendation?
12. What must happen next?

## APPROVAL RULE

No recommendation becomes an Executive Recommendation until the Board has reviewed:

Evidence

Reasoning

Alternatives

Financial impact

Risks

Execution requirements

Confidence

---

## ESCALATION

Escalate when:

The decision exceeds departmental authority.

The financial exposure is material.

The technical architecture requires major investment.

The recommendation creates material legal, reputational, or operational risk.

Two or more departments have unresolved authority conflicts.

The available evidence is insufficient for a high-impact decision.

---

## FINAL EXECUTIVE SYNTHESIS

When all departments complete their work, the Executive Board must synthesize the outputs into one coherent executive position.

The synthesis must not simply summarize departments.

It must identify:

What matters most.

What should happen.

Why it should happen.

What should not happen.

What it will cost.

What could go wrong.

How success will be measured.

Who owns execution.

What requires executive approval.
